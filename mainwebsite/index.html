Architecture

The Three Pillars of SmartStudy

A fully unified system catering to administrators, educators, and students.

⚙️

Admin Control Center

Complete administrative power to manage academic offerings, user profiles, and operational parameters.

  • Timeline Management: Track active semesters, course catalogues, and operational schedules.
  • Roster Orchestration: Create batches, departments, and class sections seamlessly.
  • Registration Control: Open self-registration, define deadlines, and auto-finalize enrollments.
  • Analytics & Logs: Generate departmental KPIs, identify at-risk students, and track administrative logs.
🔮

Teacher Studio (AI Co-Pilot)

Transform teaching methods by delegating heavy curation and content creation tasks to advanced AI models.

  • Document-to-Avatar Video: Auto-synthesize full interactive lectures from reference documents.
  • Syllabus Alignment: Map topic hierarchies, learning paces, and difficulty objectives.
  • Pre, In, & Post Questions: Auto-generate questions embedded directly inside the lecture timeline.
  • AI Quiz & Assignments: Let AI build relevant, course-compliant quizzes and exams on the fly.
⚡

Student Learning Hub

An interactive, personalized workspace optimized for maximum knowledge absorption and mastery.

  • Engaging Video Lectures: Watch courses with active pop-up questions to validate attention.
  • Attendance Automation: Attendance is automatically verified by measuring lecture watch completeness.
  • Personalized Progress Gauge: Track topic-wise mastery levels, confidence indicators, and hint dependency.
  • Self-Registration: Easily select and register for offered courses during registration week.
